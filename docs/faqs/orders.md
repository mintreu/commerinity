# Orders & Shopping FAQs

### How can I track my order?
You can track your order by going to the "Orders" section in your dashboard. This page will display a list of all your past and current orders with their current status. Click on any order to view its detailed tracking information, including the shipping carrier and estimated delivery date.

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, you can cancel an order before it has been shipped. To do so, go to the "Orders" section, select the order you wish to cancel, and click the "Cancel Order" button. If the order has already been shipped, you will need to follow the return process once you receive it.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept a wide range of payment methods, including:
- UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
- Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
- Net Banking from all major banks
- Digital Wallets
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for select pin codes.

### Can I update my ticket with new information?
Yes, you can add more information to an active support ticket at any time. Simply navigate to the "Support Center" in your dashboard, open the relevant ticket, and you can reply to the conversation to provide additional details or attachments.
