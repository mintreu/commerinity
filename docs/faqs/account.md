# Account & Registration FAQs

### How do I create an account?
To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. You will be asked to register using your mobile number. An OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your mobile to verify your account. Once verified, you can set your password and complete your profile.

### I forgot my password. What should I do?
If you've forgotten your password, click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You will need to enter your registered mobile number or email address to receive a password reset link or OTP.

### How do I update my profile information?
You can update your profile information by navigating to the "Profile" section in your dashboard and selecting "Edit Profile". Here, you can update your name, email, address, and other personal details. Some changes, like updating your mobile number, may require OTP verification for security.

### How long does it take for support to respond to a ticket?
Our support team typically responds to inquiries within 2-4 business hours. If you are a Premium member, you will receive priority support with a guaranteed response time of 1 hour.

### How do I upgrade my user status or membership?
To upgrade your status, visit the "Membership" page from your dashboard. You will see a list of available plans and their benefits. You can upgrade your membership by paying the difference using your wallet credits or any other available payment method.
