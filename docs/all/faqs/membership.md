# Membership & Network FAQs

### What are the benefits of becoming a member?
As a member, you unlock a range of exclusive benefits, including:
- Special discounts on products and services.
- The ability to earn commissions on purchases made by your referrals.
- Access to our powerful affiliate program and your own referral code.
- Priority customer support with faster response times.

### How does the referral program work?
Once you become a member, you will receive a unique referral code. Share this code with your friends, family, and social network. When someone signs up using your code and makes a purchase or subscribes to a membership, you will earn a commission based on your membership level and their activity.

### How do I check my earnings from referrals?
You can track all your commissions and earnings by navigating to the "Earnings" section in your dashboard. This page provides a detailed breakdown of your commission history, pending payouts, and total earnings from your network.
