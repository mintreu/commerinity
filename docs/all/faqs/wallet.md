# Wallet & Withdrawals FAQs

### How do I check my transaction status?
All your wallet transactions are recorded and can be viewed in real-time. To see your transaction history, navigate to the "Transactions" section of your sidebar menu or look for the transaction history within your Wallet dashboard.

### How do I withdraw money from my wallet?
To withdraw money from your wallet, go to the "Wallet" section and select the "Withdraw" option. You will need to enter the amount you wish to withdraw, select a verified bank account, and enter your transaction PIN to authorize the withdrawal. Withdrawals are typically processed within 2-3 business days.

### What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
The minimum amount you can withdraw from your wallet is Rs. 100. Please ensure your KYC (Know Your Customer) information is verified before attempting to make a withdrawal.

### How do I set up my transaction PIN?
The first time you access any of the financial features of your wallet, such as sending money or making a withdrawal, you will be prompted to create a 6-digit transaction PIN. This PIN is a secondary security measure and will be required for all future financial operations to ensure your account's security.
