# Security FAQs

### How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, go to the login page and click on the "Forgot Password" link. You will need to enter your registered mobile number or email address. A verification code or a reset link will be sent to you. Follow the instructions to set a new password for your account.

### What is the transaction PIN?
The transaction PIN is a 6-digit secondary security code that you create. It is required to authorize all financial operations on your account, including making payments from your wallet, transferring funds, and processing withdrawals. This adds an extra layer of security to your financial transactions.

### How can I make my account more secure?
We recommend the following practices to enhance your account's security:
- Use a strong, unique password that you do not use for any other online service.
- Do not share your password or transaction PIN with anyone.
- Be cautious of phishing emails and messages asking for your login credentials.
- Regularly review your transaction history for any unauthorized activity.
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) if the option is available.
