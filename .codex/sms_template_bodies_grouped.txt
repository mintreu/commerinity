SMS TEMPLATE BODIES (DLT Pattern Draft)
Source: Pattern-aligned short SMS copy


FINAL FORMATS:-

==================================================
OTP  [Final , Dont Alter]
==================================================

[otp-general]
Your OTP is {#number#} for {#purpose#}. Valid for 10 minutes. Do not share it. - {#app_name#}
DLT APPROVED FORMAT :-
Your OTP is {#number#} for {#alphanumeric#}. Valid for 10 minutes. Do not share it. - VRIDDHI VIKASH

[otp-transaction]
Your OTP is {#number#} for {#alphanumeric#} Rs {#number#}. Valid for 10 minutes. Do not share it. - {#app_name#}
DLT APPROVED FORMAT :-
Your OTP is 123456 for credit Rs 250.00. Valid for 10 minutes. Do not share it. - VRIDDHI VIKASH


OTP KEY MAPPING (USE 2 FORMAT ONLY)
otp-verification -> otp-general
login-otp -> otp-general
registration-otp -> otp-general
password-reset-otp -> otp-general
transaction-otp -> otp-transaction

[welcome]
Welcome to {#app_name#}. Your account has been successfully created. Start using our services now. - {#app_name#}

[wallet-update] [final dont change .. eta use korbo pore]
Rs {#number#} {#alphanumeric#} to your wallet. Available balance: Rs {#number#} - {#app_name#}
DLT APPROVED FORMAT :-
Rs 200.00 credited to your wallet. Available balance: Rs 500.00 - VRIDDHI VIKASH



[withdrawal-processed]
Withdrawal of Rs {#number#} processed. Bank credit shortly. Ref {#alphanumeric#}. - {#app_name#}

TXN KEY MAPPING (MEANINGFUL COMMON FORMAT)
wallet-credit -> wallet-update (action=credited, type=wallet topup/purchase refund)
wallet-debit -> wallet-update (action=debited, type=order/payment)
commission-earned -> wallet-update (action=credited, type=commission)
withdrawal-processed -> withdrawal-processed
